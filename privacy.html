Road Trip Gas Calculator

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 21, 2026

Thank you for using Road Trip Gas Calculator (the "App"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not collect.

Information We Use

The App allows you to enter the following values to perform calculations:

  • Gas price
  • Vehicle MPG (miles per gallon)
  • Trip distance

This information is used only to calculate your estimated trip cost, cost per mile, and estimated fuel usage. All calculations happen locally on your device.

No Data Collection

The App does not:

  • Collect personal information
  • Store user input permanently
  • Create user accounts
  • Use cookies
  • Use analytics or advertising services
  • Use third-party SDKs or vendors
  • Send or receive data over the network
  • Track users in any way

Data Storage

The App does not store any persistent data. Values you enter exist only temporarily while the App is in use and are cleared when you close it.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services, APIs, analytics providers, advertising networks, or external databases.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children or users of any age.

Security

Because the App does not collect, transmit, or store user data, there is minimal privacy risk associated with using it.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above.
