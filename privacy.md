# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** May 21, 2026

Thank you for using **Road Trip Gas Calculator** (the “App”). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not collect.

## Information We Collect

The App allows users to enter:

* Gas price
* Vehicle MPG (miles per gallon)
* Trip distance

This information is used only to perform calculations such as:

* Estimated trip cost in U.S. dollars
* Cost per mile
* Estimated fuel usage

## No Data Collection

The App does **not**:

* Collect personal information
* Store user input permanently
* Create user accounts
* Use cookies
* Use analytics services
* Use advertising services
* Use third-party vendors or SDKs
* Send or receive data through HTTP/network requests
* Track users in any way

All calculations are performed locally on the user’s device.

## Data Storage

The App does not store any persistent data. Information entered into the App exists only temporarily while the App is in use.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services, APIs, analytics providers, advertising networks, or external databases.

## Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children or users of any age.

## Security

Because the App does not collect, transmit, or store user data, there is minimal privacy risk associated with using the App.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the “Last Updated” date above.
